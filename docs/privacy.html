Privacy Policy

Effective Date: August 31, 2026

Welcome to NeuroOS ("we", "our", or "us"). We are deeply committed to protecting the privacy of our users, particularly neurodivergent individuals, students, and children. NeuroOS is designed with a privacy-first, local-first architecture.

1. Information We Collect

No Personal Data Collection: NeuroOS does not collect, sell, or transmit personal identification data, location data, or contact lists to external servers. All user profile preferences, focus schedules, routine boards, and memory configurations are stored locally on your device.

2. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

Our app includes a dedicated Kids Adventure Mode designed for children. In full compliance with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA):

  • We do not collect personal information from children under 13.
  • We do not display third-party advertisements in Kids Mode or anywhere in the app.
  • Access to adult settings and mode switching is locked behind a Parent Gate.

3. Camera & Microphone Permissions

Camera and Microphone permissions are used exclusively for user-initiated Guardian Link™ video sessions and Text-to-Speech (Phonics) communication. Audio and video streams are processed live on-device or peer-to-peer and are never recorded or stored remotely.

4. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or NeuroOS, please contact us via our official repository or website at https://Charitys411.github.io/NeuroOS/.
